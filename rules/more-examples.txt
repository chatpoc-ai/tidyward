# More example rules for Tidyward. Copy the lines you want into your rules.txt.
#
#   action | semantic condition | exact conditions
#
# action               keep / archive:<folder> / trash / ask
# semantic condition   plain language, judged by the model ("a screenshot", "an invoice"); * means any.
#                      The model sees only the masked name and metadata, never the real name or contents.
# exact conditions     checked locally, comma-separated, all must hold:
#                      arrived>N days  arrived<N days  opened>N days  opened<N days  never opened
#                      size>N MB  size<N MB  extension=.a/.b  has duplicate  app installed
#                      name starts with X  name contains X   (name checks use the real name, on this machine)
#                      "has duplicate" holds only for extra copies; each group keeps its earliest original.
#
# Order is priority: the first rule that applies wins. Put protective rules first.

# --- protect first: never cleaned up automatically ---
ask                          | passwords, keys, recovery codes or backup codes |
ask                          | a passport, ID card, visa or driver's licence   |
archive:Documents/Finance    | an invoice, receipt, bill or bank statement     |
archive:Documents/Insurance  | an insurance policy, benefits booklet or claim  |
archive:Documents/Forms      | a filled-in or signed application or claim form |
keep                         | *                                               | arrived<7 days
keep                         | *                                               | opened<7 days

# --- screenshots ---
trash                        | a screenshot                                    | arrived>90 days, never opened
archive:Pictures/Screenshots | a screenshot                                    | arrived>30 days
keep                         | a screenshot                                    |

# --- photos ---
archive:Pictures/Camera      | *                                               | name starts with IMG_, extension=.heic/.jpg/.jpeg
archive:Pictures/Saved       | a picture saved from the web or a chat app      |

# --- installers, archives, duplicates ---
trash                        | *                                               | extension=.dmg/.pkg, app installed
trash                        | *                                               | has duplicate
ask                          | a zip or other archive                          |

# --- everything else ---
archive:Movies               | a video                                         | arrived>30 days
archive:Documents/Archive    | a document                                      | opened>180 days
